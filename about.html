Welcome to Our Sanctuary

A Peaceful Destination Designed for Relaxation

Nestled in the heart of Gokole Village, Gokole Park & Guesthouse blends authentic local heritage with modern luxury, offering guests an unhurried escape into nature.

Our Story

Leadership & Community Roots

Founded under the direction of Mr. Mauba, Gokole Park & Guesthouse was established to create a premium hospitality experience that directly empowers the local community.

From sourcing fresh village produce—like organic farm eggs, pork, and free-range chicken—to hosting immersive Chief’s Kraal cultural tours, every aspect of our retreat is tied to the warmth and rich traditions of Gokole Village.

Tranquil Environment

Pristine gardens, quiet riverbank fishing spots, and scenic horseback trails designed for genuine rest and rejuvenation.

Rich Cultural Heritage

Guided excursions detailing local leadership, traditional customs, and the historic Chief's Kraal site.

Modern Versatility

Fully catered corporate conference facilities paired with authentic outdoor braai and leisure setups.
