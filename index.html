About Gokole Park

A comfortable base in the village

Gokole Park & Guesthouse is in Gokole Village, Itsani, Thohoyandou. Stay in a comfortable room, braai with family and friends, fish by the river, ride a horse along the village paths, or hire our conference hall. We also sell fresh eggs, pork and village chicken.
