Policies & privacy

How bookings, payments and stays work, and how we look after your information.

Booking and payment

How to book

Send an enquiry on our Enquiry page, or call or WhatsApp 063 143 8609. Your booking is confirmed once we have replied and your deposit has been paid.

Deposit and payment

  • We ask for a 30% deposit to secure your dates.
  • The balance is paid when you arrive.
  • You can pay by EFT or in cash.
  • Prices are in rand, per room per night. The price we confirm when you book is the price you pay.

Cancellations and changes

  • More than 72 hours before arrival: your deposit is refunded in full.
  • 72 hours or less before arrival: we keep the deposit.
  • If you do not arrive and have not told us, we keep the deposit.
  • You can change your dates at no cost if you tell us at least 72 hours ahead, subject to availability.

During your stay

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 10:00. If you need to arrive earlier or leave later, ask us and we will do our best.

House rules

  • Children are welcome. Please tell us how many and how old when you enquire.
  • Pets are not allowed in the rooms. Contact us first if you plan to bring one.
  • Smoking is allowed outdoors only.
  • Please keep noise down after 22:00 so everyone can rest.
  • You are responsible for any damage to rooms or property during your stay.

Events, tours and produce

Conference hall

A hall booking is confirmed with a deposit, the same as a room. Catering orders must reach us at least 48 hours before your event. Please leave the hall tidy when you finish.

Tours and activities

  • Chief’s Kraal tours and horse riding must be booked 24 hours ahead.
  • If the weather is unsafe, we will move your activity to another time or refund it.
  • Please follow your guide’s instructions at all times, especially when riding.
  • You can bring your own fishing gear or rent a basic set at Gokole Park.

Farm produce orders

Orders for eggs, pork and chicken are taken on WhatsApp every day from 08:00 to 17:00. You can collect from Gokole Park or ask about village delivery. We confirm the price when you order.

Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Gokole Park & Guesthouse looks after your personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This notice explains what we collect and what we do with it.

Who is responsible

Mr. Mauba, Director, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Contact details are at the end of this notice.

What we collect

When you use the Enquiry or Contact form, we collect your name, phone number, email address and message. On the Enquiry form we also collect the service you want, your preferred date, the number of guests and how you would like us to reply.

Why we collect it

We use it only to answer your enquiry and to handle your booking. We do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to, and we do not sell it or give it to anyone else.

Who else can see it

Our form messages are stored by Netlify, the service that hosts this website. Only our team can read the messages in our account.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry for as long as we need it to deal with your request and to keep our booking records. After that, we delete it.

Your rights

  • You can ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct it or delete it.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies and your browser

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The map on our Contact page comes from Google Maps, which may set its own cookies. While you move from the home page to the Enquiry form, the site briefly keeps your booking choices in your browser. They are forgotten when you close the tab.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe. No website or message can be made completely secure, so please do not send us card numbers or ID numbers through the forms.
