About us

Who we are

Gokole Park & Guesthouse is in Gokole Village, Itsani, Thohoyandou. A place to stay, eat and enjoy the village.

Our story

Led by Mr. Mauba, rooted in the village

Gokole Park & Guesthouse is led by our director, Mr. Mauba. We want every guest to feel welcome, and we want the village to benefit too.

That is why we sell fresh village produce such as farm eggs, pork and free-range chicken, and why we run guided tours of the Chief’s Kraal.

Peace and quiet

Gardens, quiet fishing spots by the river and horse trails, for a proper rest.

Village culture

Guided visits to the Chief’s Kraal, with local history and customs.

Space for events

A conference hall with catering, plus outdoor braai areas for gatherings.
